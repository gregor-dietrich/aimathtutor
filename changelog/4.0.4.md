  deserialization safeguard bypass vulnerabilities in JSON processing (CVE-2026-54512,
  CVE-2026-54513, CVE-2026-54514, CVE-2026-54516, CVE-2026-54517, CVE-2026-54518, and
  CVE-2026-54515 on the 3.x Jackson line)
